# Content Strategy

## Tone

Professional, technical, confident and concise.

## Principles

-   State two years of AI evaluation experience, as confirmed by the portfolio owner.
-   Describe tasks, review criteria, and observable decisions in plain language.
-   Publish performance figures only when there is a supporting report, scope, and measurement method. A figure repeated in a CV or work sample is not independent evidence.
-   Label illustrative prompts, responses, and work samples as portfolio demonstrations.
-   Publish certifications only with supporting credential records; do not infer completion dates.
-   Avoid generic marketing language.
-   Every section should reinforce expertise in AI evaluation.
